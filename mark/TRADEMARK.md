# The orangecheck mark

The orangecheck mark (the isometric check in this package) and the name
"orangecheck" are trademarks of OrangeCheck. The MIT licence covers this
package's code and files; it grants no right to the trademark.

## You may

- Show the mark on a sign-in button, link or screen whose action signs a person
  in with orangecheck, beside the words "Sign in with orangecheck",
  "Continue with orangecheck", "Sign up with orangecheck", or the same words in
  another language that keep "orangecheck" as written.
- Show it in documentation or interface copy that says your site works with
  orangecheck.

## Only as drawn

- The variants this package ships: `mono` in your text colour, `brand` on dark
  backgrounds, `tile` on any background, and the flat mono form for forced
  colours.
- At least 16px wide, with clear space of a quarter of its width around it.
- Never recoloured otherwise, stretched, rotated, split, cropped or outlined.
- Never more prominent than your own name and brand, and never in a way that
  suggests OrangeCheck endorses or runs your product.
